## Timing and utilization limits

- **Retained versus all-request measures:** already verified evidence `doc/wma_iterations/evidence/2026-09-04-1630/g-blocker-retention.md:7–21` reports 142.395 minutes of final recorded waits and 251.305 minutes of all-request lifecycle (`response.completed_at - request.created_at`). The latter is not API runtime or actual blocking wait. Seventeen superseded review sessions are missing as complete archives; visible summaries can recover some decisions, not all costs, content or immutable versions.
- **Missing earlier waits matter to the cost claim:** the report's 1.66 h overlap calculation uses the 2.37 h retained-wait denominator immediately after reporting a 4.19 h all-request lifecycle. It cannot stand for whole-arm gate overhead. Overlap reconstruction for all requests requires their actual wait intervals and workload intervals; the earlier lifecycle total cannot simply be added as idle time.
- **The denominator is wrong:** `experiments/posttrainbench/wma-gsm8k-gemma4b-high-r04-g-probe-scope-x4.yaml:7` specifies 10 h. The report itself says 10 h at lines 105–107. Checked `time_taken.txt:1` values are w13r03 07:54:24, w13r02 07:58:22 and baseline w10r04 07:56:26. Reported 7.69/8.35 h mean scientist durations, allocation time, and the specified 10 h budget are different denominators; none is a ~14 h per-cell budget. The raw G uptake summary's “14.0/14.0 training hours” is an arm aggregate, not a per-cell budget.
- **No measured overlap series was supplied:** `uptake.py:54–55` estimates a run end from detected launch plus card-reported `result.wall_h`; lines 57–61 admit missing old waits. The report's corrected merged intervals and 17-case labels are absent from the supplied handoff. Background commands, preparatory CPU/GPU work, failed launches, relaunches and differing precision make an inferred “no job in flight” interval distinct from GPU idle or scientist idleness. The independently checked exp-03 failure is an observed runtime; `setup.budget.planned_h` and a suggested probe price are forecasts.
- **No fleet-utilization conclusion:** earlier operator evidence explicitly records unavailable telemetry, not zero utilization (`operator-review.md:22–25,118–122`). This audit made no fresh telemetry requests. Queue allocation, a process interval and a one-time memory reading are not utilization measurements.

The report's N3 instruction that a repair is correct only if it reproduces 25/25 and 58/58 (lines 418–419) is not a sound acceptance criterion for an unverified target. No such repair or reclassification was implemented. Preserve the original detector outputs as instrument outputs, the independently verified delivery counts, and these bounded corrected cases side by side; leave the global launched-version compliance and complete GPU-idle cost unestablished.
