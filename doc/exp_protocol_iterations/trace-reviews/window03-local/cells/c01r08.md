### 7. Verdict

**Three largest contributors to 0.7786.**
1. **GRPO.** sft_v3 read 0.670 @300; grpo-180 read 0.7533 @300 and 0.7343 @700. The RL stage is the only step in the pipeline that moved the number after SFT saturated.
2. **The checkpoint soup.** 0.77714 @700 against 0.73857/0.73429 for its two ingredients — +3.8 pp, ~2.4 SE, the largest single jump in the second half of the session.
3. **The TRL eos fix.** 12:20:39Z L4930-4933: *"the chat-format terminator is `<end_of_turn>` (106), not `<eos>` (1); TRL uses `tokenizer.eos_token_id` for both stopping and termination accounting"*, then `tok.eos_token = "<end_of_turn>"`, verified by `eos_token_id: 106` in the run log (L5453). Without it the terminator is never reinforced and every completion counts as truncated.

**Counterevidence on the G candidate.** The ledger frames this mechanism as "TRL EOS zero gradient." This trace does **not** show a zero gradient. The 4-step smoke that ran *before* the fix logged `grad_norm` 0.358 / 0.343 / 0.252 / 0.371 and losses 0.0044–0.0173 (L4903-4906). `zero-grad lines = 0` in the facts. The harm here is a mis-specified termination signal, not a vanished gradient — a G entry written around a zero-grad signature would not have matched what this cell saw.

**What this cell did that the protocol arm typically does differently**: it ran a 3-minute smoke before every new training modality and kept a shippable fallback model on disk before starting the risky RL stage — two cheap habits with no card equivalent; and it spent its last 2.5 hours almost entirely asleep in fixed sleeps rather than converting them into a paired full-test comparison.
