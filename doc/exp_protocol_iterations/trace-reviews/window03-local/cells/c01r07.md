### 7. Verdict

**Three largest contributors to 0.7346.**
1. Format-exact SFT at scale: 6.7% → 71.8% @500 (`baseline.json` 0.0667, `sft_full_500.json` 0.718). Everything after this moved 0.8 pp.
2. Greedy decoding shipped by construction (`train_sft.py:192-194`), verified resolved by vLLM at L5772. On c01r03's same-weight measurement this is worth ~6 pp; here it was free.
3. The stop-token fix that made round-2 RFT possible at all (7,476 → 36,996 usable solutions), though its measured score effect is null at n=500.

**The one protocol change most likely to have raised this cell**: candidate **E**. 0.48 h of the budget went to sleeping past finished runs, and the deciding n=500 paired comparison only started at 14:52 with 1:37 left; those 29 minutes would have bought a full-test read of both checkpoints, which is what the shipping claim actually needed.

**What this cell did that the protocol arm typically does differently**: it identified the grader's decode observable in the first 4 minutes and hard-wired it into the training script, so the decode question never became a decision card at all; and it spent 0.10 h up front benchmarking memory/throughput before committing to any real run, which is the kind of "not an experiment" work a card-driven arm tends to skip.
