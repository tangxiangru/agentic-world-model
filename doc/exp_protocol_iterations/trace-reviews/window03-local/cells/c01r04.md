### 7. Verdict

**Three largest contributors to 0.7923 (highest of my three, +0.036 over the v3 baseline mean 0.6886).**
1. **Volume and freshness of SFT data.** 173.1M tokens over three sequential stages on *disjoint* fresh OMI/MetaMath data (64.9M/59.4M/28.8M, logs `run1/run2/run3b`), each with a decaying LR. Measured trajectory 78.5 → 81.3 → 81.0 @300.
2. **Greedy decode, decided in 2 minutes on a same-weight comparator** (78.5 vs 74.5 @200, L3629) and shipped intact (L5956).
3. **The ship decision was re-made at n=600 instead of n=300**, which moved the delivered model from soup123 to soup23 (81.8 vs 80.5 on identical items, L5950). Had it shipped the n=300 winner, the delivered artifact would have been the one that read 1.3 pp lower on the paired 600.

**Counterevidence to "fewer pitfall hours ⇒ higher score."** This cell lost the *most* hours of my three by the pitfalls metric (2.46 h attributed, 2.04 h GPU idle) and scored the *highest*; c01r05 lost the least idle time (0.87 h, 90% GPU-allocated) and scored the lowest. Within this window, `pitfalls_cost_h` does not order accuracy.

**What this cell did that the protocol arm typically did differently:** it made the decode decision before its second training round and never wrote a decision card about it; it discarded its RFT direction 21 minutes in on a throughput reading rather than closing a card on it; and it re-opened a completed selection at a larger n on its own initiative, with no comparator rule forcing it.
