## Group notes (outside the reports)

These are observations that span the three cells I was given. They are not decisions, and they do not settle anything about the eight-cell cohort — g01s01/03/04/06/08 are under other reviewers and the planner combines all eight.

**1. E fails in all three of my cells, by 7×, 8× and 18×, and the dominant mechanism is not the one E's text was written against.** Cumulative post-exit idle: g01s02 1.13 h, g01s05 1.13 h, g01s07 2.72 h. Only 0.67 h (g01s07), 0.48 h (g01s02) and 0.25 h (g01s05) come from a dead process nobody polled. The remaining **2.05 h + 0.65 h + 0.60 h = 3.30 h** is waiting past *evaluations that had already finished* — sleeps of 6–38 minutes for runs that took 7 seconds to 3 minutes. Every one of those durations was printed as a `total time:` line in a log the scientist was tailing. E's frozen `while kill -0 <pid>` loop would cap each at one 300 s poll if generalised to evaluation processes; its literal wording ("wait for the run … chain the evaluation to the run's exit") speaks to trainings. Whether that generalisation happens is exactly what a screen would measure — and it is a wording question, not a reason to withdraw the candidate.

**2. Producer-exit timestamps in these bundles are exact, and cheap.** Every `evaluate.py` log ends with a `[W903 hh:mm:ss.…] destroy_process_group` line at process exit and carries a `total time:` line; training progress bars give elapsed seconds that anchor to any wall-clock tail. Future E accounting should use these rather than file mtimes or GPU memory, both of which the metrics note already warns against. All the exact rows in my three tables come from this source; the estimated rows are marked with bounds.

**3. D is hit twice, cleanly, by cells whose parents were greedy-patched — and once by a path D's check does not cover.** g01s05 exp-05 (1.47 h of failed compute) and g01s07 exp-03 (1.17 h) both name `setup.parent_checkpoint.path` pointing at a checkpoint whose `generation_config.json` is greedy, which is exactly what `parent_generation_config_valid` reads. g01s02's same-validator failure came from its own `soup.py` setting `do_sample=False, temperature=0.0` in memory before `save_pretrained`, with a stock-config parent — the check would have passed it. Cost there was only ~0.04 h because the artifact was a 40 s merge.

**4. `pitfalls_cost_h` double-counts in g01s07.** exp-03 and exp-04 both carry a `cost_h: 2.0` entry for the same crash, so `collect` reports 4.20 h where the distinct loss is 2.20 h. Any screen that reads "hours attributable to mechanism X" needs to de-duplicate the follow-up card's retrospective entry. The same shape will recur whenever a failed card is re-run as a new card, which the protocol encourages.

**5. `comparator_same_protocol` is the check these cells route around, and it does so invisibly in one of them.** g01s02 overrode it twice, each time because the comparator arm is produced by the card's own launch. g01s07 hit the same FAIL on exp-05 and resolved it by launching the comparator eval first and locking afterwards — so the successful lock records `fail: 0`, `overrides: {}`, and `collect` sees nothing. `preflight_fail` counts only failures recorded in a written lock; a failure resolved by reordering work is not counted.

**6. H's evidence differs by an order of magnitude across three cells that all report `n_overrides` ≈ 0.** g01s07 created `eval/README_eval_only.md` whose text reads *"This file exists only so that setup.data can point at a real path, as the card schema requires"*, used it on 4 of 7 cards, and passed with 0 overrides and 0 failures because a `.md` path makes three jsonl-specific checks `skip`. g01s02 has 7 truthful-but-inapplicable entries and 1 override. g01s05 has 1 honest provenance reference. Counting overrides alone would rank these in exactly the wrong order.

**7. P1: no confirmed hit in these three, one partial, and one instructive false positive.** g01s02's RFT trained from base on a 68%-teacher mixture and shows a steep descent (0.653 at step 10) — fails the lineage clause outright. g01s05's exp-03 satisfies the lineage clause (hardlinked same-weights parent) but is a 58/42 self/teacher mixture, and its step-20 loss of 0.3323 is **0.073 from the parent's terminal logged loss 0.2589** (outside "about 0.05") while being **0.032 from the parent's whole-run mean 0.30066** (inside it) — the predicate's verdict flips on the terminal-vs-average distinction, and the card reports the mean as `final_loss`. g01s07 has no self-distillation at all, yet its exp-04 — 120,000 fresh 405B teacher rows, zero self rows — matches the loss signature perfectly (step-20 0.3277 vs parent terminal 0.3049) and returns the parent. The lineage clause is what separates them; the screening shorthand "flat first-step loss" does not. All three retained logs use `logging_steps=20` or `10`, so the step-20 read the entry prescribes is available in every case — "not printed in the trace" is not "unobservable" here either. Recoverable hours from a step-20 stop in these cells: **≈0.20 h in g01s05 and nothing in the other two**; sampling time cannot be counted.

**8. G has zero exposure across all three: 0 GRPO launches, 0 RL cards.** Two cells reject RL with a time-and-setup argument (g01s02: *"no preference or reward pipeline exists and ~5.5 h remain"*; g01s05: DPO needs *"a reference-model pass and careful beta tuning"*), and g01s07 never mentions it. That is non-exposure, not a success and not a baseline G could move.

**9. Guard-specific observables are clean in all three.** Hook blocks 0, false blocks 0, locked-open cards 0, live scientific work lost at session end 0 (GPU verified empty and process lists empty in every case), 11/11 training launches after their card's lock. The one ordering irregularity is g01s07's two *evaluation* launches before their locks — one caused by `comparator_same_protocol`, one self-reported as a rule-1 deviation — which the training-launch observable does not see. Three cells is not the cohort; the planner owns that read.

**10. P5 keeps growing and stays confounded.** Same weights, same items, different run: g01s02 0.7005 vs 0.6907 at n=1319 with only 51.9% byte-identical completions; g01s05 0.7733 vs 0.740 at n=150; g01s07 0.776 vs 0.770 at n=500. Same shipped artifact, developer vs official: g01s02 965 vs 956 of 1319 (max_connections 32 vs 2); g01s07 991 vs 970 of 1319 (16 vs 2). The developer/official gaps are 1.5–3.5× the within-config jitter, and serving config, process identity and repeat variance vary together in every one of them. g01s07 separately measured the first-500 prefix to run ~2 points above the full set, which is the cleanest quantification of that bias I found in these traces.
