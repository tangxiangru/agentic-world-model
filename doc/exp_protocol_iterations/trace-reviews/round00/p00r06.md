Three largest contributors to 0.7210:
1. **Greedy decode baked in at 13:01, before any result** (positive; L2998–L3007, L4716). It is why this cell, with an otherwise ordinary SFT chain, is the best of the three.
2. **No on-policy RL, and ≈2.7 h on zero-or-negative bets** (negative): DPO/GRPO rejected at 6.1 h and 4.7 h left as not fitting "alongside a final-model verification" (L6212, L7661), then listed as the untested lead at the end (L9793); meanwhile exp-03's crash (0.63 h), the RFT round (1.3 h incl. sampling, −0.2) and the Orca-Math epoch (1.4 h, −3.2) consumed the hours a control cell gave to GRPO (+0.75 to +8).

The shipped exp-10 gain (+0.76 full-set) was correctly labelled "a defensible pick … not a demonstrated improvement".

One protocol change most likely to have raised this score: a `pitfalls.yaml` entry `greedy_generation_config_breaks_trainer_save` (symptom: training runs to the end, every `save_pretrained` raises `GenerationConfig is invalid`, no weights survive; cause: transformers validates `do_sample=false` + `temperature=0.0` on save; guidance: reset `model.generation_config` before training, write the greedy JSON after the final save, smoke-test one save). Its 0.63 h is one more candidate — and the trap is a direct consequence of the decode fix the protocol should be recommending, so the two entries belong together.

Versus a strong control cell (SFT on OMI-2 with few-shot prefixes → greedy → GRPO → full-test selection): same on greedy and on full-test selection; a smaller prefix share (6 % vs 12–30 %); the same SFT-on-OMI-2 core but stacked as three SFT epochs on partly-fresh data instead of one SFT stage plus GRPO; ran to 0:51 left like the controls, but the last 3.5 h went to Orca-Math, verification and a second epoch rather than to on-policy RL.
