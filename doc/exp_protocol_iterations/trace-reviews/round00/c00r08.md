## 7. Verdict

Largest contributors, with evidence:
1. **Greedy decoding: +8.4 pts** (62.3 → 70.7 at n=300, L3833), decided at 12:49 and applied to every checkpoint.
2. **Two-stage SFT on OMI-2 with fresh data + on-policy RFT rows**: 70.7 → 73.7 (n=300) / 74.75 (n=800).
3. **GRPO, but under-budgeted**: +2.0 at n=300 / +0.75 at n=800 from 165 steps × 128 rollouts ≈ 21k rollouts; the continuation plateaued. c00r06 spent ~130k rollouts for +5 at n=500. Two structural reasons: 15% of RL prompts carried the 2k-token 10-shot block (`--p-fewshot 0.15`, `max_prompt 2600`) and the generation batch was 128 with bs 8 × accum 16, giving ~57 s/step; and the SFT v1 started 4.7 pts below c00r06's (bf16 weights, eff. batch 64, GSM8K human CoT mixed in).

Knowledge worth a rule in the protocol arm's pitfalls list:
- "The container root overlay is 64 MB and may already be full: never `uv pip install --system`; install to `./pylibs` with `--target` and set `PYTHONPATH`. A full overlay produces zero-filled `.py` files that fail only when first imported." (L4768 → L4982; c00r02 hit the same wall at 12:31 and pre-empted it.)
- "After killing any vLLM job (`pkill`, crash, timeout) run `nvidia-smi --query-compute-apps=pid,used_memory` and `kill -9` every leftover `VLLM::EngineCore` before relaunching; otherwise the next engine dies with 'EngineCore failed to start' and the queue silently stalls." (L4276 → L4317; ~15 min here, same pattern in c00r06 at 14:20 and c00r07 at 13:53/15:13)
- "For GRPO on one H100 budget rollouts, not steps: keep RL prompts free of the 10-shot prefix and use `generation_batch_size ≥ 192` so a step costs ≤ 30 s; 165 steps at 128 rollouts is not enough to move GSM8K by more than ~1 point." (L5634 vs c00r06 L6259)
