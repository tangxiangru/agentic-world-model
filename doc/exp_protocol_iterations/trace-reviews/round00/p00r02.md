## 2. Recipe decisions and reasoning

## 7. Verdict

**Three largest contributors to 0.776:**
1. **Greedy decode config (+14.0 at n=150, positive, largest).** Found 4 minutes into the first training run by dumping the request body (L4895–L4898, 12:55:43), tested as an isolated card at 14:50 (L5842), carried into every later checkpoint and asserted by `verify_final.py`. This is the single decision that separates this cell from p00r01 (0.580) and p00r03 (0.680), neither of which wrote the file.
2. **Data volume (positive).** 127k grader-shaped rows for the first SFT (0.0867 → 0.6133) and a disjoint 135k slice as a continuation (+4.0, 13 fixed / 7 regressed, L7728); the scientist's own summary: "the only two interventions that moved the number were greedy decoding and more data" (exp-06 card, L8020).
3. **exp-06's silent death (negative, ≈1.8 h).** The greedy config the scientist had adopted crashed the next Trainer save; two long blind sleeps (L8228, L8274) hid it for 1.28 h of idle GPU. The last 2.6 h therefore yielded a rejected 48k continuation (−3.3) instead of either the full 95k slice or the planned n=500 re-ranking of exp-05 vs exp-03 vs the intermediate checkpoints. The pick (exp-05 at n=150) turned out fine, but it was made on a 4-point gap with stderr 0.033.

**The ONE protocol change most likely to have raised this score**: a `pitfalls.yaml` entry with a mechanical preflight check, `greedy_generation_config_breaks_trainer_save` — "symptom: run dies at the first checkpoint save with `GenerationConfig is invalid … do_sample False … temperature 0.0`; cause: transformers ≥4.5x validates generation_config on save and the parent carries the greedy file; check: `parent_generation_config_valid` (read `setup.parent_checkpoint.path/generation_config.json`, FAIL if do_sample false with temperature set); guidance: sanitise in the trainer and write the greedy json after the final save." It would have fired at exp-06's lock (18:57:35) and returned ~1.8 h — enough for the n=500 selection the scientist had queued (L7568) and for the 95k slice. A second candidate with similar value: a reminder "poll for process liveness, not log tails" (the tail at 19:55 already showed a stale progress line).

**Versus a strong control cell** (SFT on OpenMathInstruct-2 with few-shot prefixes → greedy config → GRPO → full-test selection): this cell did the first two well (prefix on 10% of rows, greedy config at 14:49), replaced GRPO with a one-shot RFT that was null, and never evaluated beyond n=150 — it shipped exp-05 without ever seeing a full-test or even n=500 number, and left the exp-02/exp-05 intermediate checkpoints unscored.
