## 7. Verdict

Three largest contributors to 0.7043:
1. **Greedy decode, found at 12:37 and measured +16.0 at n=150** (positive; L2952–L3090, L4773, exp-03). This is the whole gap to p00r04 (0.684, sampling shipped).
2. **Recipe ceiling** (negative): every SFT was a single epoch (exp-02 1 × 50k; exp-05 1 × 116k), a second epoch was rejected in favour of unique data (L9111), and DPO/GRPO was rejected twice on schedule grounds (L8018, L9115). The 2.3 h exp-05 retrain-from-base bought +1.2 at n=500 (p=0.56) over exp-03; the RFT round bought −0.7/−1.0. Controls got +0.75 to +8 from GRPO in the same hours.
3. **Stopped at 6.3 h with 3.7 h unused** (negative) on the "cannot be measured" argument above, which rested on a 10x overestimate of the full-test eval cost; and ≈1.1 h lost to recoverable mistakes (eval defaults 0.25, gen_rft crash 0.6, orphan/shards/soup 0.25).

One protocol change most likely to have raised this score: guidance in `evaluation.protocol` (and a `check`-able WARN) that the declared n must make the stderr smaller than the delta the hypothesis claims, and that a final selection or a stop-with-budget-left decision is scored on the full test set — with the note that a full eval here costs minutes. That removes the two noise-driven rejects and the stated reason for stopping; a runner-up is a rule that ending with more than ~1 h left requires a card stating what was not tried and why.

Versus a strong control cell (SFT on OMI-2 with few-shot prefixes → greedy → GRPO → full-test selection): matched on greedy (7/7 controls) and on prefix share (12 % vs 12–30 %); differs in the SFT depth (one epoch, no second pass), in never trying on-policy RL (5/7 controls did), in never scoring the full test set (controls up to n=1344), and in stopping at 6.3 h while controls ran 8.2–9.2 h.
