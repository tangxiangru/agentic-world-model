Three largest contributors:
1. **+7.0 greedy decode** (measured on its own card, shipped, verified from a fresh process) - the single reason this cell sits 5-8 points above the other two protocol cells. The protocol's card format made the measurement clean but did not prompt it: no pitfall, check or reminder mentions decode defaults.

One protocol change most likely to have raised this score: a `pitfalls.yaml` entry `decode_defaults_from_generation_config` (check: null -> printed reminder) with guidance "evaluate.py passes no temperature; vLLM takes do_sample/top_k/top_p from the model dir's generation_config.json (gemma-3 ships do_sample true, top_k 64, top_p 0.95). Ship `do_sample: false` and delete the sampling keys - do not set temperature 0.0 / top_k -1: transformers' save_pretrained validates strictly and the next continuation dies at its first checkpoint." For this cell it returns ~2.1 h (enough for the filtered RFT or a full-test ranking); for p00r09/p00r10 it is worth +5-7 points.

Versus a strong control cell: SFT on OpenMathInstruct-2 with few-shot prefixes - yes (8% exact 10-shot prefix); greedy config - yes, found unaided at 1.7 h; GRPO - no, never mentioned; full-test selection - no (n=500 on the unshuffled head). The biggest divergence is four sequential "more external data" cards where a control cell would have moved to an on-policy stage.
