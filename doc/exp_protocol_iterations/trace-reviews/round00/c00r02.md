## 7. Verdict

Contributors to 0.6846:
1. **One-epoch OMI-2 SFT shipped greedy** (68.0% at n=300; graded 68.5%) — decode config was right from 12:38, data was decon-clean, and the artifact was packaged by `finalize.py` before the session died.
2. **The safety-net copy at 14:40:08** (`cp -r work/sft_v1 final_model`, L6499718): without it the killed session would have shipped nothing.
3. **Nothing after SFT ran.** The RFT round (est. +5 pts by analogy with c00r07's 59 → 66) and any further training were lost to the session ending at 2.4 h; the SFT itself was weaker than c00r06's (68.0 vs 75.4) — half the optimizer steps (451 vs ~640), ≤2 solutions per problem, bf16 weights, extra MATH ballast — but that is inference from configs, not a measured ablation.

Knowledge worth a rule (these are harness-interaction rules, and they apply to the protocol arm just as much):
- "Never send an assistant message without a tool call while any job is running: in `--print` mode a text-only reply is `end_turn`, background tasks are killed and the sandbox is torn down. Wait with a foreground `sleep N` (N < tool timeout) or `while kill -0 PID; do sleep 60; done`, and keep polling until the job has finished." (L8162236 → Result block; 7.6 h lost)
- "One waiter per job: never launch another background `sleep` while one is pending; each background task adds a notification and a `background_tasks_changed` listing to the context — 1,300 of them here cost ~$260 and forced a compaction." (L46543–L54704; L8160476)
- "A detached pipeline script must finish the job on its own: compare, copy the winner to `final_model`, write the report — the agent may not be alive when it ends." (`run_v2.sh` stops at `bash run_eval.sh work/sft_v2 300 v2_greedy 32`; the replace-if-better step was left to the agent, L8162236)
