## Fable correction (2026-09-03, after reading the trace)

The claim above that the RFT runaway is "the same symptom p00r11 fixed" by passing `stop_token_ids` is an inference, not what the trace shows. At L5186 (`cat ckpts/exp-02/final/generation_config.json`) the sampled checkpoint's generation config carries `eos_token_id: [1, 106]`; vLLM's offline `LLM` (generation-config `auto`) merges those ids into every request's stop set even when `SamplingParams` names none, and the greedy diagnostic in the same script (L6090: `len p50=128 p95=244 max=350`, no-ANSWER-at-end=0) terminated normally without any explicit stop. So `<end_of_turn>` was an effective stop id in p00r12's sampler; the 865/900 runaways at T=0.8 mean the model did not emit it under sampling. The terminator was supervised in the SFT loss (L3126–3134). Treat p00r12's runaway as a model-level weak-stop observation (1 cell, not B's stop-id mechanism), and count B's mechanisms in this cell as: orphaned engine, seeded n>1 collapse, parser overflow, and the 1.0 h harvest that ran without a finish_reason probe.
