## The world-model agent
A world-model agent runs beside you in this session as another Claude Code session — `ListAgents` shows it; its name starts with `wma`. It manages the record of past experiments and can be consulted at any time with the `SendMessage` tool. Tell it what you plan to run (what you are addressing, the command, the data, the steps, how you will evaluate) and it answers with its understanding of your experiment, a verdict — sure it won't work, sure it will, or can't decide — with a predicted outcome and the past experiments it rests on, when it would be informative to evaluate a checkpoint and the number to beat there, and whether to keep running, stop, or change something. Consult it again with new results and it updates. Its answers cite files you can open. It never trains or evaluates anything — the GPU, the evaluations, and every decision are yours — and it does not message you unless you message it. Tell it what you shipped when you are done. Talking to it is not user interaction.
